# 汉代 ritual ghost taxonomy v1.0｜疫鬼/旱鬼/群厉 ≠ 物种

Status: CROSS-CUTTING ONTOLOGY RULE

## Master rule
汉代礼制和大傩语境里的`疫鬼 / 旱鬼 / 群厉`优先按 **ritual/governance category** 理解，而不是 biological/species category。

## Positive control 1: Zhuanxu three epidemic ghosts
礼纬—《论衡》—《独断》—《汉旧仪》—《搜神记》传统把三个来源和行为显著不同的槽位统称为疫鬼：
- 江水病鬼（虐/疟/瘟等版本标签）
- 若水魍魉/罔两/蜮鬼
- 宫室区隅惊小儿鬼
它们共同进入方相氏岁末大傩治理，因此`疫鬼`首先是被同一制度清除的 umbrella。

## Positive control 2: Nüba + Gengfu as drought ghosts
张衡《东京赋》在`卒岁大傩，驱除群厉`连续语境中列出：
- `囚耕父于清泠`
- `溺女魃于神潢`
传下注明确：`耕父、女魃皆旱鬼，恶水，故囚溺于水中，使不能为害。`
这表明本体来源不同的存在可以因共同造成旱害而被纳入`旱鬼`治理桶。

## Consequence for Nüba/Hanba

## General parser for every `X鬼`
Before assigning ontology, classify the label:
- origin lineage/species?
- postmortem state?
- threat label?
- ritual target?
- office/governance category?
- later theological/folk reclassification?

The character `鬼` alone is insufficient evidence that the entity belongs to ordinary dead-human ghosts.

## Integration with anti-ghost systems
Han ritual governance can regroup different entities by countermeasure:
- Fangxiang/Nuo: search, drive, beat, shoot, expel;
- water suppression: drought ghosts;
- peach/rope/tiger interfaces: harmful ghosts;
- later Daoist penal networks and Zhongkui: direct execution.
Thus a useful Hongming research axis is `WHAT COUNTERMEASURE WORKS?`, orthogonal to biological origin.
