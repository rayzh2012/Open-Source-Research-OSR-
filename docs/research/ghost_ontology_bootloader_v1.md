# 鬼类 Ontology Bootloader v1.0

Bootloader snapshot: 2026-08-26
Notion: `3c82a508-97fd-814f-bf48-e604d7ad40ec`

## Master rule
`鬼` is not a single species. Classification must split orthogonal axes: origin, substrate/state, identity integrity, threat, belonging, hunger, corpse-ness, predation, and ritual/governance target.

## Control cases
### Epidemic ghosts
The Zhuanxu-three-ghost tradition contains three different ontology slots—river disease ghost, Ruoshui wangliang, household child-fright ghost—under one Nuo governance umbrella. Therefore `EPIDEMIC_RITUAL_TARGET != SPECIES`.

### Drought ghosts
Separate `early Nüba divine layer → Han ritual drought-ghost classification → later drought-ba abnormal entities → late corpse-ba`. Han ritual classification does not prove the early deity was an ordinary dead ghost.

### Huangfu
Keep `CHIGUO_ALIAS_HUANGFU` and `HUANGFU_PESTILENCE_GHOST` separate unless a real bridge appears.

## Anti-ghost dual poles
- Chi/Chi-Guo: `HUMAN/BIOLOGICAL SUBSTRATE → GHOST PREDATOR`.
- Zhong Kui: `HUMAN → DEATH/GHOSTIFICATION → RETAINED HUMAN IDENTITY → GHOST-SUBSTRATE EXECUTIONER`.
They converge on anti-ghost function but are not declared genealogically identical.

## Comparative technical-history model
`Fangxiang/Nuo → masks/Qitou/ritual weapons → Daoist ghost-execution networks → Zhong Kui killing function → later persona/image standardization` is allowed as a functional comparison, not a proven direct evolution.

Rejected: all ghosts one species; evil ghost implies low cognition; epidemic ghost = infectious-disease species; drought ghost = original ontology of early Nüba; Huangfu ghost = ChiGuo by name alone; ChiGuo = ZhongKui ancestor; Qiongli six eyes belong to Zhuanxu’s three epidemic ghosts.
